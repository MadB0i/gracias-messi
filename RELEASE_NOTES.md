# v1.0.0 — Gracias, Messi

A fan tribute to Lionel Messi's international career, written on notebook
paper — with a wall for everyone else to leave their own note.

**▶ [Live demo — open it, no install needed](https://madb0i.github.io/gracias-messi/)**

What's inside:

- **Wall of Gracias** — leave a line for the 10. No server: your note becomes a GitHub issue and the wall is the repo's own issues.
- **Where Were You** — type your birth year; the timeline shows your age at every milestone, or "Not born yet."
- **Generate my recap** — a ~15-second vertical video of the stats, trophies, and timeline, drawn and recorded entirely in your browser. Nothing uploads.
- **Page-turn bio** — five chapters that flip like notebook pages (scroll, swipe, arrow keys, or the dots).
- **Crowd ambience** — opt-in, fully *synthesized* stadium swell (Web Audio, no samples) that rises past the big moments.
- **EN / ES / HI** — one tap switches the prose; the numbers stay.

…plus a self-drawing "Gracias, Argentina," a sketched-in doodle of the 10,
animated counters, a 22-season chart + heatmap, shareable PNG cards, a
3D gallery, a `/` command palette, and a GOAT easter egg (type it).

Every figure and quote is verified and attributed — the full method is in
[SOURCES.md](https://github.com/MadB0i/gracias-messi/blob/main/SOURCES.md).

---

*No frameworks, no backend, no build step — vanilla HTML/CSS/JS.*
