## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-02

First public release of the tribute.

### Added

- A handwritten "Gracias, Argentina" that draws itself in, stroke by stroke, beside the closing note.
- **Wall of Gracias** — a guestbook with no server: your note becomes a GitHub issue and everyone's notes are read back from the repo.
- **Where Were You** — enter your birth year and the timeline shows your age at each milestone (or "Not born yet").
- **Generate my recap** — a ~15-second vertical video of the stats, trophies, and timeline, drawn and recorded entirely in your browser.
- **Page-turn bio** — the five story chapters flip like notebook pages (scroll, swipe, arrow keys, or the dots).
- **Crowd ambience** — an opt-in, fully synthesized stadium swell that rises as you scroll past the big moments.
- **EN / ES / HI** — a language switch for the headlines, section titles, and milestone captions.
- A hand-drawn margin doodle of the 10, sketched in beside the final note.
- Animated stat counters, a 22-season goals chart, a contributions-style heatmap, and downloadable PNG stat cards.
- A 3D parallax gallery of the real places and objects, a trophy case, and a career timeline.
- Interactive touches: ink scroll progress, chapter dot-nav, a `/` command palette, and a GOAT easter egg.
- An embeddable 200px widget and `messi-career.json` with every verified stat.

### Fixed

- The closing note's handwriting no longer overlaps: the layout now comes from the browser's real text, with the ink drawn as a measured overlay that fades to the real lettering.
- Replaced broken stock images with real, credited photos (Rosario, Newell's, Camp Nou, the European Cup, Lusail, Argentina jerseys).
- Rephrased attributed quotes to reported speech and labeled the retirement note as a paraphrase.

### Docs

- Added `SOURCES.md` documenting where every quote and number comes from.
- Restructured the README to describe the full feature set and linked the live demo and sources.
- Disclosed the gallery photography with per-photo license and author credit.
