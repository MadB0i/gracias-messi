# Sources

Quotation policy for this tribute: direct quotes are used **only** for (a)
Messi's own words published on his official biography, and (b) the retirement
note, which is always labeled as a paraphrase. Everything attributed to other
named people (Rexach, Gaggioli, Piqué, Fàbregas) is written as reported
speech, not direct quotation.

## Reported speech (paraphrased from named people)

These are paraphrases, not verbatim quotes. Each is widely reported; the
places where the underlying statements are commonly reported:

- **Carles Rexach** — said he saw enough in two minutes of Messi's trial and
  told the board the club had to sign him immediately. Commonly reported via
  FIFA.com's 25th-anniversary feature "Lionel Messi lands in Barcelona"
  (Sept 2025) and numerous outlets covering the 2000 signing.
- **Horacio Gaggioli** — described 13-year-old Messi on arrival at El Prat as
  exceptionally small, and said he doubted he could play football. Commonly
  reported via the same FIFA.com feature and press coverage of the
  25th anniversary.
- **Gerard Piqué** — said the La Masia group had written the kid off before he
  played, until he touched the ball. Same FIFA.com feature.
- **Cesc Fàbregas** — recalled that teammates wondered how the small,
  near-silent boy would hold his own. Same FIFA.com feature.
- **The napkin contract wording** — the exact text of Rexach's napkin
  agreement is reported in the FIFA.com feature; this page paraphrases it
  rather than reproducing the wording, since the primary document text cannot
  be independently confirmed.

## Historical facts

Standard biographical facts (birth, Rosario, Newell's, growth hormone
diagnosis, 2000 trial, La Masia, 2004 debut, 2009 first Ballon d'Or, 2014/2016
heartbreaks, 2021/2022/2024 triumphs, 2026 retirement) follow Wikipedia's
Lionel Messi article, Britannica, FIFA.com, and FC Barcelona / Argentina FA
records.

## Career data

All numbers in `messi-career.json` are verified totals as of August 31, 2026.
Season-by-season goal counts are approximate widely-reported career totals.
Month-level goal records are not in the public record; the goals heatmap
distributes each season's verified total across its twelve months and labels
this on the page.
